Transportation · Pre-loss intelligence

Nu Alert™

A pre-loss signal layer for road transportation.

Nu Alert™ by SYGNL Technologies is a patent-pending, connectivity-independent system designed to identify defined vehicle and driver-state risk signals at the edge, before they become loss events. It does not diagnose medical conditions, build biometric profiles, or collect personal health data. Human judgment remains in the loop.

  • Category: transportation risk intelligence
  • Positioning: Black Box for road transportation
  • Model: audit, pilot, licensing and subscription

Water · Community resilience intelligence

Water Signal Tracker™

When the water stops, the signal should not.

Water Signal Tracker™ by SYGNL Technologies is a community water disruption reporting and resilience intelligence platform. It turns public reports of no water, weak pressure, intermittent service, and restored flow into location- and time-based evidence that communities, utilities, researchers, and decision-makers can use.

  • Category: civic water intelligence
  • Tracks disruption, pressure, intermittency and restoration
  • Designed to complement, not replace, utility systems

Culture · Caribbean event discovery

WeyDePartyDey™

Find the vibe. Feel the culture.

WeyDePartyDey™ by SYGNL Technologies is a Grenada-based Caribbean event discovery platform for locals, visitors, and diaspora audiences. It brings together fêtes, bands, mas, food, services, hotels and stays, tickets, and live Carnival updates in one searchable experience.

  • Category: Caribbean events and cultural discovery
  • Primary market: Grenada and Spicemas
  • Built for visitors, locals, promoters and businesses
