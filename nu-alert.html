Nu Alert™

Nu Alert™ by SYGNL Technologies is a pre-loss signal layer for road transportation. It is designed to help fleets recognize emerging operational risk early enough for a person to act.

What Nu Alert does

Nu Alert supports a Detect → Alert → Intervene workflow. It is designed to work at the edge, remain useful when connectivity is limited, and keep people in control of decisions.

What Nu Alert is not

Nu Alert is not a medical device and is not positioned as a biometric or personal-health monitoring system. It does not rely on personal health records. Its focus is driver-state and operational signals, fleet policy, and timely human intervention.

Who it is for

Commercial fleets, transportation operators, safety leaders, insurers, and organizations seeking stronger pre-loss and first-notice-of-loss visibility.

Why it matters

Many systems document what happened after an incident. Nu Alert is built around the earlier question: what operational signals were present while intervention was still possible?

Available entry points

SYGNL offers fleet risk reviews, a 60-Day Fleet Signal Audit, pilot discussions, and FNOL-focused workshops.
