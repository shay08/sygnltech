Water Signal Tracker™

Water Signal Tracker™ by SYGNL Technologies is a community water-disruption reporting and resilience intelligence platform built in Grenada.

What it does

Residents report whether water is unavailable, weak, intermittent, or flowing normally. Those reports create a clearer picture of outage duration, pressure conditions, restoration gaps, and community impact across locations.

Why it exists

Official restoration and household usability are not always the same thing. A system may be reported as restored while some communities still experience no water, weak pressure, or intermittent flow.

What makes it different

Water Signal Tracker treats community experience as operational evidence. Its Community Impact Duration™ measure focuses on how long people remain affected, not only when a repair was declared complete.

Who it is for

Communities, water utilities, disaster and resilience agencies, researchers, development partners, and governments seeking better visibility into water-access disruptions.

Positioning

Water Signal Tracker is not built to criticize a utility. It is designed to add a missing visibility layer between infrastructure events, service restoration, and lived community conditions.
