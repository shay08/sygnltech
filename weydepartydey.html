WeyDePartyDey™

WeyDePartyDey™ by SYGNL Technologies is a Grenada-based Carnival and culture discovery platform for locals, visitors, and the diaspora.

What it helps people find

Fêtes, bands, mas, food, local services, hotels and stays, tickets, live Carnival updates, and other experiences connected to Grenada’s Carnival season and wider cultural life.

Why it exists

Visitors and locals often discover events through scattered flyers, private messages, social media posts, and word of mouth. WeyDe brings that information into one place so people can decide where to go and what is happening.

Who it serves

Residents, returning nationals, regional travelers, international visitors, promoters, bands, vendors, hotels, service providers, and Carnival communities.

SheaShea™

SheaShea™ is WeyDe’s digital Caribbean concierge, designed to help people explore events, food, travel, nightlife, local experiences, and practical Carnival information.

Official positioning

WeyDePartyDey is a discovery and cultural-intelligence platform. It is not an official government or Carnival authority website.
